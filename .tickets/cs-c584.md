# Derive future value from the entry's best remaining path

Future value and DILI rest on FV_MID, FV_WIDTH, SURVIVE, the three STYLE values, four calendar weights and SCARCITY_P, none derived or scored. The cost of spending team t now is the drop in the entry's best remaining season when t is removed, which an assignment over the remaining legs computes directly and which makes holiday feasibility exact rather than a dock.

## Design

Maximize the sum of log win probability over the remaining legs with each team once, infeasible holiday assignments excluded. Cost of t = best path value minus best path value without t. Far-future win probabilities shrink toward 0.5 with horizon. Shown beside DILI before it replaces it.

## Acceptance Criteria

Computed for CIRCAmcised-2 every week alongside DILI, parity with brute force on a small synthetic season, and the nine constants deleted once it replaces DILI.
