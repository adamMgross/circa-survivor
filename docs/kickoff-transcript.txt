### **Survivor Contest Overview**

* NFL Survivor: pick one team per week, they just need to win; can’t reuse a team  
* Hosted by Circa Sportsbook in Vegas, $1,000/entry, 25,000+ entries, $25M pot  
* Requires physical presence in Nevada to enter picks (gambling, not fantasy)  
* Payout split among all survivors at season end; fewer survivors \= bigger individual payout  
* Three entries this season, each person owns 5%, Jeremy holds \~55%  
* 20 pick weeks total: standard weeks plus split Thanksgiving and Christmas windows  
  * Christmas/Thanksgiving weeks split into two sub-periods, each requiring a separate pick  
  * These act as gauntlets: must have an eligible team available for each sub-period

### **Pick Strategy and the Dashboard**

* Three pillars for pick selection:  
  * Win probability for the current week  
  * Future value (how useful a team will be in later weeks)  
  * Pick popularity (how many others in the pool are choosing the same team)  
* Popularity matters because surviving alongside the whole pool doesn’t improve EV; surviving a mass elimination does  
  * Illustrated by the final-week example: if 4,999 of 5,000 entries pick the Chiefs (85% favorite), picking the Dolphins is the higher-EV play despite lower win odds  
  * Strategy must be recalibrated weekly based on pool results, not solvable a priori  
* Win probability source: consensus live betting lines (median of middle two across top sportsbooks via the Odds API)  
  * Live lines beat all models including ESPN, FiveThirtyEight, and NFL Elo  
* Future value: combination of power rankings and forward-looking odds (formula needs review)  
* Pick popularity: modeled using future value, win percentage (exponentiated to reflect herding behavior), and remaining team availability in the pool  
* Pool availability data released weekly by the casino as a tweet with a photo; currently entered manually
